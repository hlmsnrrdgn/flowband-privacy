FlowBand

Privacy Policy · HSE Studio

Overview

FlowBand ("the App") is developed and maintained by HSE Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it on your Android device.

FlowBand does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All preferences are stored locally on your device only.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • Your location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to our servers
  • Any financial or banking information

How the App Works

FlowBand displays a scrolling news and information ticker on your screen. To provide this functionality, the App makes outbound requests to the following public services:

  • News feeds (RSS): Publicly available RSS feeds from Turkish news outlets
  • Weather data: Open-Meteo API (open-meteo.com) — no account or personal data required
  • Prayer times: Aladhan API (aladhan.com) — city name only, no personal data
  • Financial data: Public market data APIs
  • Sports news: Publicly available RSS feeds

These requests are made directly from your device to the respective services. HSE Studio does not act as an intermediary and does not receive or log any of this traffic.

Local Storage

The App stores your preferences (such as selected city, chosen news sources, display settings, and personal notes) locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to HSE Studio or any third party.

Permissions

FlowBand requests the following Android permissions:

  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW (Draw over other apps): Required to display the ticker band on your screen.
  • INTERNET: Required to fetch news, weather, prayer times, and financial data.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Required to automatically restart the ticker band after your device reboots, if you have enabled this feature.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Required to keep the ticker running in the background as a foreground service.
  • REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS: Requested optionally to prevent the system from stopping the ticker band due to battery optimization.

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit personal data.

Third-Party Services

The App connects to the following third-party services to retrieve publicly available data. Each service has its own privacy policy:

  • Open-Meteo: open-meteo.com/en/terms
  • Aladhan Prayer Times API: aladhan.com/privacy-policy

HSE Studio is not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services.

Children's Privacy

FlowBand is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.
